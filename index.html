01 · PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

From Sketch to Sample to a Factory-Ready SKU

We turn a concept, a competitor's listing, or a rough spec sheet into a sourceable, compliant product — matched to the right manufacturer, engineered for cost, and ready for private label.

Concept & Feasibility

Market and cost feasibility review before a single supplier is contacted.

Supplier Matching & Vetting

Shortlisted against capability, capacity, and compliance history — not just price.

Sample Development & Iteration

Structured sample rounds with tracked revisions until spec, look, and function align.

Private Label & Packaging

Brand-ready packaging, inserts, and labeling built for Amazon and retail requirements.

Tooling & Cost Engineering

Mold and BOM review to protect margin before volumes are committed.

IP & Compliance Filing

Trademark, UL listing, and certification prerequisites mapped in from day one.

The Development Sequence

02 · QUALITY CONTROL

Every Shipment, Verified Before It Sails

Third-party-grade inspection and testing coordination across the production cycle — so problems are caught at the factory, not at your warehouse.

IEC 62619 UL Listing CB Scheme CE Marking UN38.3

Incoming Material Inspection

Raw materials and components checked against spec before assembly begins.

During-Production Inspection (DPI)

Mid-run checks that catch drift before it reaches the full batch.

Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI)

AQL-based sampling of finished goods, with photo-documented reporting.

Factory Capability & Social Audit

On-site review of a supplier's process controls, capacity, and labor practices.

Lab Testing Coordination

Samples routed to accredited labs for IEC, UL, CB, and CE test reports.

Container Loading Supervision

Verified quantity, packing condition, and container seal at load-out.

Standards We Inspect & Test Against

IEC 62619:2022 UL Multiple Listing CB Scheme CE / RED UN38.3 Transport MSDS
