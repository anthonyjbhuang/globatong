Quality Control — FAQ

What clients ask before booking an inspection.

A pre-shipment inspection (PSI) checks the finished goods themselves — usually AQL sampling right before the order ships. A factory audit reviews the supplier's processes, capacity, and labor practices, independent of any single order.

Depends on the product and destination market — commonly IEC 62619, UL listing, CB Scheme, CE/RED, UN38.3 for transport, and MSDS documentation. We confirm the applicable set with you before testing begins.

Yes — samples are routed to accredited labs and we manage the paperwork and follow-up between the factory and the lab so certification doesn't stall in email threads.

Both matter for different reasons. Pre-shipment inspection catches product defects before packing; container loading supervision separately verifies quantity, packing condition, and seal at load-out.

Consolidation & Logistics — FAQ

What clients ask before booking freight.

FCL (full container load) books an entire container for your cargo alone. LCL (less-than-container load) shares a container with other shippers' cargo, which usually costs less for smaller volumes but takes a bit longer to consolidate and deconsolidate.

Yes — that's the core of the service. Cargo from several vendors is picked up and pooled at a consolidation hub before a single shipment moves to port, which typically lowers the freight cost per unit.

Yes — FNSKU labeling, poly-bagging, and carton configuration are built to Amazon's current requirements before the shipment moves onward.

All three are available depending on how much of the customs and duty process you want handled on your behalf. DDP hands you a landed, cleared shipment; FOB leaves origin-side handling to us and destination clearance to you.

Supply Chain Intelligence — FAQ

What clients ask about the reporting layer.

Open POs and shipment status, landed cost per unit, vendor performance scorecards, and reorder timing — pulled from the same records used to run development, QC, and freight for your account.

Data can be delivered as scheduled reports, through an API, or via EDI depending on what your team already uses — we fit the reporting to your existing stack rather than asking you to change it.
